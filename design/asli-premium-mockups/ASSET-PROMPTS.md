# Aslı App — Uygulama görselleri

Üretim aracı: yerleşik ImageGen. PNG dosyaları şeffaf arka planlıdır. Uygulama metinleri ve kontrolleri görsellere gömülü değildir; Flutter tarafından çizilir.

Dosyalar:

- `mobile/assets/illustrations/learning-book.png`
- `mobile/assets/illustrations/achievement-medal.png`
- `mobile/assets/illustrations/patient-shield.png`
- `mobile/assets/illustrations/clinical-heart.png`

Kitap ve madalya ortak istemi:

```text
Create a production-ready isolated raster illustration asset for the Aslı App Flutter UI. Use the supplied mobile mockup as a STYLE reference only. No interface, no text, no labels, no border or background panel. A genuinely TRANSPARENT background with alpha. Square canvas, centered object with 8% clear padding; it must remain legible at 120px. Match the lovely matte clay 3D materials, soft studio lighting, ivory, lavender, apricot and sage palette of the mockup.
```

Kitap eki (referans: 02-home.png):

```text
Subject: an open ivory book viewed slightly from above, with a sculptural lilac ribbon rising vertically from its center toward a floating peach sphere, three tiny ivory sparkle rays. Match the hero book sculpture in the reference. Keep the silhouette compact, beautiful soft shadows confined under the object, no backdrop.
```

Madalya eki (referans: 06-quiz-result.png):

Subject: one elegant apricot achievement medallion with a raised ivory four-point spark in its center, lilac folded ribbons hanging below, two tasteful sage laurel sprigs framing the base.
